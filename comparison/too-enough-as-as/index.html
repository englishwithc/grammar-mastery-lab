Step 9 · Your poster

You built it. Here it is.

Put your name on your grammar poster and read it out loud one more time.

GRAMMAR POSTER FOR

1
TOO + adjective = more than we want.
The soup is too hot. · The glass is too full. · The bag is too heavy.
2
adjective + ENOUGH = just right.
The soup is warm enough. · The key is big enough. · The glass is full enough.
3
NOT + adjective + ENOUGH = less than we want.
The key is not big enough. · The jacket is not warm enough. · The glass is not full enough.
4
AS + adjective + AS = the same.
Tom is as tall as Sara.
5
NOT AS + adjective + AS = different.
Tom is not as tall as Sara.
