THE COUNTING KITCHEN · GRAMMAR CARD

How much or how many?

1

COUNTABLE — we can count it.

One apple, two apples, three apples. Plural nouns take -s.

2

UNCOUNTABLE — we measure it.

Milk, rice, bread, cheese. No -s. We say some milk, not “two milks”.

3

The two pairs — learn them together.

uncountablecountable (plural)
wordmuchmany
verbisare
exampleHow much milk is there?How many apples are there?
negativeThere isn't much milk.There aren't many apples.
4

Short answers

There is a lot of milk. · There are six apples. · There isn't much rice. · There aren't many cookies.

TIP a lot of works with both: a lot of apples, a lot of milk.
