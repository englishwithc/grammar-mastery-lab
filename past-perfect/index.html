Quick Reference

Past Perfect Simple

had + past participle
  • Action completed before another past action
  • States with duration up to a past point
  • With: already, just, never, yet, before
  • Focus: the result, the action is complete
"She had finished when I arrived."

Past Perfect Continuous

had + been + verb-ing
  • Action in progress before another past action
  • Explaining a past state (cause)
  • With: for, since, all day, how long
  • Focus: the duration, the activity itself
"She had been working for 3 hours when I arrived."
