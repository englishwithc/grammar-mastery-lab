🔊 The -ed ending has three sounds. Spelling tells you nothing about the sound, so listen!

/t/

After hard sounds: p, k, f, s, sh, ch

walked · watched · stopped

/d/

After vowels and soft sounds: l, m, n, v, z, b, g…

played · lived · loved

/ɪd/

After t or d sounds (it adds an extra syllable)

wanted · studied · needed

Tip: if the verb ends in /t/ or /d/, you hear an extra "id" sound.

/t/

walked · watched · stopped

/d/

played · lived · loved

/ɪd/

wanted · studied · needed

👻 The past of "to be" is WAS or WERE. There is no -ed and no did!

👻 With be, we do NOT use did. "I didn't was happy" is wrong. ✅ "I wasn't happy."
👻 Was she happy? · Were they at school? — never "Did she was…?"

🎲 Irregular verbs don't follow the -ed rule. Their past forms must be learned, but they follow patterns.

No change

cut → cut
put → put
hit → hit

Add -t

build → built
send → sent
feel → felt

Vowel change

eat → ate
sing → sang
see → saw

-ew → -ewn

grow → grew
know → knew
throw → threw

Special

go → went
have → had
be → was / were
