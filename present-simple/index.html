⚠️ Careful! "She play" ❌ → "She plays" ✅. The verb needs an -s with he, she and it.

⚙️ For he, she and it, the verb gets an ending. Which ending? Three rules explain it.

Rule 1: +s

play → plays

most verbs, also vowel + y

Rule 2: +es

watch → watches

after s, sh, ch, x, o

Rule 3: -ies

study → studies

consonant + y

Special

have → has

learn this one by heart

👻 "To be" is special! I am, you are, he is. The verb is NOT a regular Present Simple verb.

Tap the pink block to change the complement.

📌 With be, the verb changes with the subject. It has no -s and it never takes a second verb.
⚠️ Common mistakes
"I is happy" ❌ → "I am happy" ✅
"She am happy" ❌ → "She is happy" ✅
"He is play football" ❌ → "He plays football" ✅ (is + verb is wrong)

❌ Negatives use don't or doesn't + the base verb. The verb never changes!

Formula: Subject + don't / doesn't + base verb
I / you / we / they → don't · he / she / it → doesn't

❓ Questions start with Do / Does (or Am / Is / Are). The subject comes next.

Be questions:
Am → I · Are → you / we / they · Is → he / she / it
⚠️ After Do / Does, the verb is ALWAYS base form: "Does she like?" ✅ · "Does she likes?" ❌
Also: "Am you happy?" ❌ → "Are you happy?" ✅
