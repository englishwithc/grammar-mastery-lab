One S per sentence

  • he · she · itthe verb takes one S — She plays.
  • I · you · we · theyplain verb, no S — They play.
  • ch · sh · s · x · o → -eswatch → watches, go → goes
  • consonant + y → -iesstudy → studies, carry → carries
  • vowel + y → just -splay → plays, buy → buys
  • doesn't / doesthe S lives in there, so the verb is plain — She doesn't play. Does she play?
  • have → hasHe has a dog. Never "he haves".

Say every answer out loud. The mouth learns the S before the brain does.
